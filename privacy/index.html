Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy covers the website revgrs.github.io and REVGRS Publisher, the tool REVGRS uses to publish its own music clips to its own accounts on TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube. REVGRS is an independent music project.

Who uses REVGRS Publisher

Only REVGRS. The tool is not offered to the public: no one else can sign in to it, and it never acts on anyone else's account.

What data the tool handles

When the REVGRS account authorizes REVGRS Publisher on TikTok, TikTok shares the following about that account, and only that account:

  • basic profile information (account ID, display name, avatar);
  • account statistics (follower, following, like and video counts);
  • the list of the account's own videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comments, shares).

The tool uses this to publish REVGRS's music clips and to see how those posts perform. It does not collect data about viewers, followers or any other TikTok user.

Storage and sharing

  • Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the REVGRS owner's own computer, in files readable only by that user.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Data is kept while the authorization is active. Revoking access (TikTok: Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions) stops all access; the stored tokens are then deleted.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement. Links to YouTube, Bandcamp, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and the merch shop lead to services with their own privacy policies; purchases in the shop are handled by Fourthwall.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a message to @revgrs.synth on Instagram.
